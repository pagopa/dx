# Aggregate analytics extract

Synthetic, privacy-safe aggregate data. No person-level records are included.

- Source: Permit appointment aggregate dashboard, reporting period
  2026-07-01–2026-07-31.
- The dashboard counts 1,200 appointment-change support contacts.
- The extract does not provide an appointment-change completion baseline or an
  adoption baseline.
- Data & Insights has not confirmed a target or assigned an owner for future
  outcome measurement.
- The Service Access PM proposed reviewing aggregate contact and completion
  measures monthly. This proposal is not an approved KPI plan.
