# Constraints — synthetic held-out initiative

- Each reconciliation must preserve the source-record reference.
- Product has not confirmed a target reduction in manual effort.
- Data & Insights has not confirmed a measurement owner or baseline.
- Engineering and Legal/Privacy have not assessed the proposal.
- A Design/Experience artifact is confirmed not applicable because the
  initiative is internal, backend-only, and has no changed user interface.
- The reconciliation should not silently merge records with conflicting
  identity attributes.
