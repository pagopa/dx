# Product brief — synthetic initiative

This scenario is fictional benchmark material, not a real municipal program or
legal requirement.

- Initiative: let residents change an existing municipal permit-renewal
  appointment online.
- Confirmed sponsor: Citizen Services Director.
- Confirmed product owner: Service Access PM.
- Current problem: residents call Customer Care when they cannot change a
  booked appointment without staff help.
- Confirmed scope: explore self-service appointment changes. The supported
  appointment types and cutoff period are not yet decided.
- Desired outcome: residents complete eligible appointment changes with less
  effort while existing appointment records remain accurate.
- The sponsor's planning proposal permits a resident to request a change until
  12 hours before the appointment, without a fee. This is not an approved
  policy.
- The Director asked Product to develop the opportunity, not to approve or
  launch it.
