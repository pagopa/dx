The monorepo generated by `dx init` lints with Oxlint and formats with Oxfmt

The scaffold no longer generates or installs ESLint or Prettier artifacts: `nx.json` infers the `lint` target from `@nx/oxlint` (running with `--fix` locally and only verifying in CI), the root ships an `oxlint.config.ts` that extends the `@pagopa/oxlint-config` preset and no longer ships a `.prettierignore`, and the bootstrap installs `@nx/oxlint`, `oxlint`, `@pagopa/oxlint-config` and `oxfmt`. `AGENTS.md` documents the new toolchain.
